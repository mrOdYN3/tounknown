# toUnknown — Marketplace Strategy & Membership Model {#tounknown-marketplace-strategy-membership-model}

**Companion files:** *Design Doc* (look & feel) · *Coding Doc* (build spec). This doc is the business layer: how the community earns, what members and teachers pay, and the order to build it in. ## 1 · The model in one breath {#1-the-model-in-one-breath}

A **spiritual community marketplace** for video and audio courses. Members pay one membership for everything; teachers are members too — they pay to belong, and earn from what they teach. toUnknown stops renting its community from Patreon and owns the whole relationship.

### Positioning: Insight Timer for the roots {#positioning-insight-timer-for-the-roots}

Insight Timer is an ocean — 150,000\+ tracks from anyone, ancient and pop\-wellness side by side. **toUnknown is the opposite: a curated temple.** Only practices with documented ancient roots enter the catalog — Vipassana & Anapanasati (Pali Canon), the 112 techniques of Vigyan Bhairav Tantra (Shaiva tradition), Advaita Vedanta self\-inquiry, Bhakti Yoga, Stoic contemplation (Marcus Aurelius). The founder's own courses are the template for what belongs.

This becomes a hard product rule with three faces:

1. **Every course cites its root** — a "Lineage" line on every course card and page (tradition \+ source text \+ era), e.g. "Vigyan Bhairav Tantra · Kashmir Shaivism · \~8th c." This is also an SEO/AEO gift: entity\-rich, unique content no generic meditation app has.
2. **Teachers apply with their lineage** — the application asks: which tradition, trained by whom, which source texts. No lineage, no seat. Curation *is* the moat.
3. **Browse by tradition** — alongside level/audience filters, a Traditions row: Buddhist · Tantra/Shaiva · Vedanta · Bhakti · Stoic. The catalog reads like a living library of humanity's contemplative roots, not a feed.

Tagline direction for this positioning: *"The ancient paths. Guided."* or *"Only the roots."*

## 1b · The Gurukula model — the upgrade over Calm, Headspace, Insight Timer & YouTube {#1b-the-gurukula-model-the-upgrade-over-calm-headspace-insight-timer-youtube}

The depth pivot changes the economics, and they get *better*, not worse. Mass wellness apps fight brutal churn (novelty fades in weeks) and buy every user with ads. Committed seekers are the opposite market: they already pay $500–2,000 for physical retreats, they stay for years because real paths are multi\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\-year, and they arrive through sangha word\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\-of\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\-mouth — near\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\-zero acquisition cost. Proof this works: Waking Up thrives at \\\\\\\\\\\\\\\~$130/yr on depth alone with a "money should never be the reason" scholarship; Goenka centers run entirely on dāna; and cohort\-based courses (the Maven model) command premium prices because self\-paced content completes at only 5–15%.

toUnknown's model is not "a smaller Calm." It is a **digital Gurukula** — a progressive school of the ancient traditions — built from four inversions of the existing apps:

| Existing model | Its disease | Our inversion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube / Insight Timer: infinite free library | Choice paralysis, zero depth, teachers earn pennies per play | **One Path at a time.** No feed, no browsing binge. You choose a lineage Path (Vipassana · Tantra · Vedanta · Bhakti) and walk it. Commitment is the product. |
| Calm / Headspace: flat sub for content quantity | Content is commoditized — YouTube gives it free | **Tuition ladder by depth of guidance,** not content volume. Money follows the teacher relationship, which cannot be commoditized. |
| Self\-paced courses everywhere | 5–15 % completion kills transformation and word\-of\-mouth | **Cohorts:** dated starts, 20–30 walk together with a teacher. Completion multiplies; premium price is justified; teachers earn real income. |
| All of them: everything visible to everyone | Depth becomes a scroll\-past commodity | **Earned depth (initiation gates).** Advanced parts stay invisible until unlocked by practice — hours sat \+ a written reflection \+ a gate sit. "Old student" status, like Goenka retreats. Your practice record becomes identity — and an unswitchable moat. |

The dāna engine stays as the soul: milestone giving after practice, and a visible **scholarship rule — "no one is turned away"** — which (as Waking Up shows) *increases* paid conversion rather than cannibalizing it.

### Why this is defensible {#why-this-is-defensible}

Calm cannot copy this: their UX exists to minimize commitment. Insight Timer cannot copy it without firing their ocean. It requires what only you have — lineage curation, the founder's own practice credibility, and a community small enough to feel like a sangha. The moat is not the audio files; it is the Path record, the gates, and the teacher bond.

### Scope: all ancient traditions, seeded by four {#scope-all-ancient-traditions-seeded-by-four}

The school is not limited to the founder's own courses — it is a home for **every ancient contemplative tradition**, each held to the same lineage standard. Launch with the four living Paths (Vipassana · Tantra · Vedanta · Bhakti, taught by DYN), and display the full map from day one as **"Paths awaiting their teachers"**\: Zen (Zazen/Shikantaza), Tibetan (Dzogchen/Mahāmudrā), Rāja Yoga (Patañjali's Sūtras), Sufi (Dhikr/Muraqaba), Taoist (Zuowang), Stoic (Marcus Aurelius/Epictetus), Hesychast (Prayer of the Heart/Philokalia), Kabbalistic (Hitbodedut). Each empty Path is itself a recruiting engine — a visible, named seat with an "awaiting its teacher · apply" line — so the catalog grows the supply side while signaling the full vision to students. One authorized teacher opens one Path; 2–3 per Path maximum keeps the scarcity that makes a seat worth $25/mo.

## 2 · The tuition ladder (seekers → students → sādhakas) {#2-the-tuition-ladder-seekers-students-sdhakas}

Tiers follow **depth of guidance**, not content quantity — content is the invitation, guidance is the product:

| Tier | Price | What it opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Seeker** | $0 / dāna | First gate of every Path (3 tracks), tasters, community read access |
| **Student — toUnknown\+** | **$11 / mo\\\\\\\*\\\\\\\* or \\\\\\\*\\\\\\\*$88 / yr** | All Paths (unlock by practice), downloads, community participation, monthly all\-hands satsang |
| **Sādhaka — the guided circle** | **$33 / mo** | Everything above **\+ a small circle (max 30) with a teacher**\: monthly live small\-group guidance, direct Q&A, accountability, gate reviews. This tier is the profit center and the transformation engine. |
| **Live retreats & cohort intensives** | **$108–$330** one\-time | Dated online retreats (weekend to 10\-day) and cohort runs of the Paths — the high\-ticket layer serious students already buy elsewhere for 5–10× the price |
| **Founding Member** | one\-time $108 (limited) | Lifetime Student tier, gratitude wall |

The "give more if it feels true" slider stays on every tier. **Scholarship rule, stated publicly: no one is turned away for money** — apply by writing one honest paragraph; it builds the trust that converts everyone else.

Revenue sanity check at committed\-niche scale (not mass scale): 300 Students ($3,300) \\\\\\\+ 60 Sādhakas ($1,980) \+ 20 teacher seats ($500) ≈ \\\\\\\*\\\\\\\*$5,780 MRR\*\*, plus \~4 retreats/yr × 80 seats × $150 avg ≈ \\\\\\\*\\\\\\\*$48k/yr\*\* — roughly **$115k/yr** total with streaming costs under $2k/yr. Every number scales with depth of trust, not ad spend.

## 3 · Teachers (the Sangha Circle) {#3-teachers-the-sangha-circle}

Teachers are practitioners first. Positioning the fee as *community membership that includes a teaching seat* — not a "listing fee" — is what makes $25/month feel fair.

- **Sangha Circle seat: $25 / month** (your number — keep it). Includes: listing in the catalog, teacher profile page, video/audio hosting, promotion in newsletter & socials, access to everything a toUnknown\+ member gets, and the members\-only teachers' circle.
- **Founding teachers: first 3 months free** — you need supply before demand; recruit 10–15 aligned teachers before public launch.
- **Quality gate:** every teacher and course is approved by you (DYN). Curation *is* the brand — this is not Udemy.

### Revenue share — hybrid, teacher\-generous {#revenue-share-hybrid-teacher-generous}

| Stream | Split (teacher / platform) | Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One\-off course sales & course\-level donations | **80 / 20** | Stripe Connect, automatic split at payment |
| Membership pool (the $11s) | **70 / 30** of the pool, divided by **minutes watched/listened** per teacher's content that month | Skillshare\-style engagement share, paid monthly |
| Teacher's own dāna link on their profile | **90 / 10** | Direct tips, near\-pass\-through |

Why hybrid: a flat fee **plus** a greedy rev\-share kills supply. $25/mo covers your fixed costs per teacher (hosting, payout ops); the generous 80/20 and 90/10 splits make toUnknown clearly better for teachers than Udemy (37–75% platform take) and competitive with Gumroad\-style 10%. The 30% on the membership pool funds the platform's growth — that's the stream you own.

### Cohort economics — the teacher recruiting weapon {#cohort-economics-the-teacher-recruiting-weapon}

On Insight Timer a popular teacher earns cents per play from a shared pool. Here, a teacher running one **cohort** — 25 students × $150 for a 6\\\-week guided Path intensive — earns \\\*\\\*$3,000 at the 80/20 split\*\*, twice a quarter if they wish, plus their share of Sādhaka circle fees for circles they guide. That single sentence is the recruiting pitch that pulls authentic lineage teachers out of the ocean. Scarcity is enforced by design: each Path has at most 2–3 authorized teachers, so being accepted *means* something — and earns accordingly.

25 teachers × $25 \= $625/mo base before a single member. 500 members × $11 \= $5,500/mo → \\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\~$1,650 platform share \+ $625 fees ≈ \\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\*\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\*$2,275/mo platform revenue\*\* at a modest scale; video streaming for that audience costs roughly $50–150/mo (Bunny/Cloudflare Stream). ## 3b · Format focus & the Path model (what to sell in 2026) {#3b-format-focus-the-path-model-what-to-sell-in-2026}

Market reality: meditation apps are a $2.1 B market (2025) growing \~15 %/yr toward $8.7 B by 2035; freemium dominates (62 % start free, then convert); anxiety/stress relief is the \#1 use case (56 %), with sleep the fastest\-growing content segment; spiritual wellness apps specifically grow \~14.7 %/yr.

### Focus order — audio first, on purpose {#focus-order-audio-first-on-purpose}

1. **Audio courses (the core product).** Meditation is an eyes\-closed medium — video adds nothing at the moment of practice and costs \~10× to produce and stream. Audio is downloadable, offline, repeatable, and it's what you already have. Insight Timer's entire teacher economy runs on audio for this reason. **Sell structured audio journeys, not loose tracks** — structure (Day 1 → Day 5, Part 1 → Part 11) is what people pay for; loose tracks are discovery, not revenue.
2. **Video (the trust layer, not the practice layer).** Short teacher videos — face, lineage, one teaching — sell the course; monthly live satsangs justify the membership. YouTube stays the free funnel. Don't build a video course library first.
3. **Music, mantras & soundscapes (the retention layer).** Highest\-repeat, lowest\-production content; keeps daily opens alive between courses and rides the sleep\-content wave (OM chanting, mantra loops, drone soundscapes fit the roots positioning perfectly). Add in Phase 2.
4. **Sleep versions of existing practices** — a "Yoga Nidra / before\-sleep" edit of courses you already have is the cheapest way into the fastest\-growing segment.

### The pay\-per\-track question — answered by inversion {#the-pay-per-track-question-answered-by-inversion}

Charging money per track mid\-course puts a paywall at the exact moment a habit is forming — the practice moment — and would quietly kill completion, retention, and the dāna spirit. But the instinct behind the idea is right: **progression should be earned one track at a time.** So invert the currency:

**The Path model — "Unlock by practice. Give by gratitude."**

- **Enter once:** membership ($11/mo) or a single course price/donation opens the Path
- **Tracks unlock by practice, not payment:** Day 2 opens only after Day 1 is completed (a quiet gold seal: "opens when you have sat Day 1"). Scarcity through readiness — exactly how real retreats and lineages work, so it *is* the roots positioning, gamified without feeling gamified
- **Dāna at milestones:** the giving prompt appears *after* completed practice — end of Day 5, end of a Part — "If this served you, give what feels true." Post\-practice is the peak\-generosity moment; this converts far better than a paywall before practice
- **First 3 tracks of every course free** (the proven serial\-content hook: taste → trust → one decision to enter), and completing any free taster ends with one quiet line: "The Path continues with toUnknown\+"

Result: one payment decision (low friction), per\-track *engagement* (their idea, kept), rising completion rates (which drive the membership\-pool payouts fairly for teachers), and giving prompts placed where generosity is actually felt.

## 4 · Community (the glue) {#4-community-the-glue}

- **Circles**\: topic spaces (Vipassana, Tantra, Kids, Teachers) with a calm feed — text \+ audio notes, no infinite scroll, no algorithmic outrage
- **Live satsangs**\: monthly live video sessions with teachers (members only); recordings join the library
- **Practice streaks** shown gently (a breathing dot, not a guilt\-badge); **gratitude wall** instead of likes
- Phase 1 shortcut: private Telegram group as the community while the in\-app feed is built

## 5 · Build phases (each phase ships something sellable) {#5-build-phases-each-phase-ships-something-sellable}

**Phase 1 — now (static, no backend · \~days).** The app \+ admin already specced. Membership \= Stripe **subscription** Payment Link ($11/$88) \+ private Telegram \+ email delivery of content. Teachers onboarded manually via a Typeform\-style application; their courses added through the Admin Studio (author field). Patreon link removed everywhere → replaced by "toUnknown\+ Membership".

**Phase 2 — gated library (\~weeks).** Supabase (magic\-link auth, Postgres, storage) \+ Stripe Billing webhooks → members log in, library unlocks in\-app. Video: **Cloudflare Stream or Bunny Stream** (signed URLs, \~$1/1000 min); audio: R2/Bunny storage with signed links. The app grows a Player: elegant full\-screen audio player \+ video lesson view.

**Phase 3 — true marketplace (\~1–2 months).** **Stripe Connect Express** for teacher payouts (automatic splits, tax forms handled by Stripe). Teacher dashboard: upload (direct\-to\-Stream), earnings, engagement minutes. Submission → your approval queue in an upgraded Admin. Membership\-pool payouts computed monthly from playback analytics.

## 6 · Policies to write before Phase 3 {#6-policies-to-write-before-phase-3}

Teacher agreement (content ownership stays with teacher; license to stream; 30\-day exit), refund policy (7\-day, dāna\-spirit generous), content standards (authentic lineage practices, no medical claims), and payout schedule (monthly, $25 minimum). Stripe Connect handles KYC/1099s.
