## Access model — verified end to end

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Free course, no account | plays (HTTP 206, seekable) |
| Paid course, signed out | 401 — "sign in to sit this track" |
| Paid course, signed in, no membership | 402 — "this course opens with membership" |
| Paid course, active member | plays |
| Direct hit on the audio file path | 404 — Nginx `internal`, never publicly routable |

Audio URLs are minted per request and carry the session token; the file itself is served by Nginx
via `X-Accel-Redirect`, so links can't be shared or hotlinked.

Unlocking follows *abhyāsa*: a track opens only after the one before it is finished; completions are
written to `unlocks` + `practice_events`, so progress follows the member across devices.

## Design refinement (3 Aug 2026) — local only, not yet deployed

Run it: `python3 devserver.py` → http://localhost:4173/webapp/ui_kits/app/

**Luno** (thin-stroke icons, pill chips, editorial restraint), **heymessage**
(cinematic imagery, two-tone headlines, text resolving out of mist). Brand
colours and fonts are unchanged — paper-white, ink, one temple gold, Marcellus +
Albert Sans.

New files, all additive — the original design system is untouched:

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `tu-refined.css` | glass materials, elevation pairs, motion curves, the mist reveal, two-tone lede, range slider |

Signature moves:
- **Liquid glass** — translucent surfaces with backdrop blur, a specular top rim
  (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.92)`) and paired contact + ambient shadows.
- **Floating tab bar** — a glass pill detached from the bottom edge; the active tab
  gets a gold lozenge. It refracts whatever scrolls beneath it.
- **Mist reveal** — content rises *and* de-blurs (`blur(10px) → 0`) instead of sliding.
- **Two-tone lede** — the phrase that matters stays ink, the rest recedes to 58% —
  measured at 4.3:1 on paper, so it passes AA.
- **Frosted card base** — a blurred plate under card text keeps titles legible over
  cover art that already carries its own typography.
- **Blurred-backdrop cover art** — sheet headers show any aspect ratio whole,
  on a blurred copy of itself, rather than cropping.

## Round 4 — enforcement, reach, protection (3 Aug 2026)

**Unlock by practice is now actually enforced.** Previously the browser decided a track was
"sat" when the audio element fired `ended` — draggable to the end in a second. Now:

- `/api/audio` records a `listen_sessions` row the moment a signed-in member starts a track.
- `/api/unlock` grants the unlock only once **real elapsed time** ≥ 80% of the track's duration
  (minimum 30s). No telemetry to spoof — it is wall-clock.
- `INSERT` on `unlocks` is revoked from `authenticated`; only the service key writes.

Verified against the live server: unlock before starting → **409**, immediately after starting →
**425 "still sitting, 768s remaining"**, direct client write → **403 permission denied**.
If someone scrubs, the sheet says so rather than silently refusing.

**Reach.** The kids' courses now have their own section on Home ("For children"), not just a
heading below five lineage Paths. Cover switched from the Christmas artwork to the Kewin cover
so it works year-round.

**Legal.** `webapp/legal.html` — a short, plain terms & privacy page covering what is stored and
why, the membership/refund position, the scholarship line, and the not-medical-advice caveat.
The footer no longer points at `tounknown.com/terms-of-use`, which would have 404'd at the DNS flip.

**Backups.** `pg_dump` (PostgreSQL 17 client) runs nightly at 03:17 → `/srv/backups`, gzipped,
14 days retained. Audio has Google Drive as its master; member accounts, practice history and
unlocks had no second copy until now.

**Balance.** The course switcher was over-signalled — gold border, gold stepper, gold label all at
once. Now a neutral hairline, a quiet stepper, and gold on the single word "Change".

### A note on the outage during this round
Adding `const member = …` inside PathSheet shadowed the existing `member` state variable. Babel

## "Where to begin" — an intake for newcomers (3 Aug 2026)

Four questions, one screen at a time, in the brand's voice — no scoring, no quiz language, no
urgency. Members only, because the answer is kept with the practice: signed-out visitors see the
invitation and an honest reason why an account comes first.

**The questions** — have you sat before · what brings you · which door feels open · how long can
you honestly sit. The *door* decides the tradition (breath→Vipassana, senses→Tantra,
inquiry→Vedanta, heart→Bhakti, reason→Stoic); *what brings you* breaks a tie. The first course
offered is the gentlest available: a taster, else a free course, else the first.

Stored on `members` as `intake` (jsonb), `suggested_path`, `intake_at` — writable by the member
themselves, still under the existing row-level security. Mārga then marks that Path
*"suggested for you"* and the button changes to "Answer again — find another starting place".

The result screen closes with the important line: *"Nothing is locked to this. Every Path stays
open — this is only where we would start you."*

Verified end to end with a temporary account (since deleted): never / something heavy / the heart
/ ten minutes → **The Bhakti Path**, begin with *Whispers of God*; row written correctly; Mārga
marked it. Entry points sit under the Home hero and at the top of Mārga.

## The practice discount — practice lowers the price (5 Aug 2026)

The model now matches the philosophy. A subscription that is indifferent to whether anyone sits
contradicted *"unlocked by sitting, never by paying"*; sitting now reduces what the next month costs.

**Tiers** — 20 days · 25% · 25 days · 50% · every day · free. Counted at the **end** of the month,
so there is no streak to break and a missed day costs nothing. That distinction is deliberate:
streak mechanics punish the person who misses a day, which is the opposite of what a practice
should do to someone.

**A practice day** is a calendar day on which a member *completed a guided sitting*. Talks and
introductions do not count — the discount follows practice, not listening. Derived in
`practice_days` / `practice_month` from `practice_events` joined to `tracks.is_sitting`.

**A free month can be given away.** `POST /api/practice/gift` turns a 100% reward into a code the
member can hand to someone who cannot pay, and refuses a second attempt on the same month. This
keeps dāna in the money and not only in the copy — and it is cost-neutral, because the member is
choosing to give rather than keep.

**The monthly job** (`/opt/tu-api/practice-discount.js`, cron 04:23 on the 1st) closes the month
that has *ended*, records the reward, and applies a Stripe coupon to the next invoice. Coupons are
created once per tier and reused. `--dry-run` and `--period` for testing.

**Verified end to end**: 22 days → 25% with 25 shown as the next tier; 30 days → 100%; the job
recorded the reward; gifting returned `TU-RV0PFX`; a second gift on the same month was refused.
Test data removed.

### Not finished — say so plainly
**A gift code cannot yet be redeemed.** `POST /api/practice/gift` mints it, but nothing consumes it.
Until redemption exists the code is a promise the software cannot keep — the same fault as the
gate reflections nobody can read. Redemption needs: an endpoint that validates an unused code,
marks it `redeemed`, and grants the recipient a month; plus a field to enter it.

## Gift codes close the loop — and fund the scholarships (5 Aug 2026)

The gift code could be minted but not redeemed, which made it a promise the software could not
keep. Redemption now exists, and with it a second option that turns the whole thing into a
self-funding system.

**A practitioner who earns a free month has three choices:**
1. Keep it — the discount applies to their own next invoice.
2. **Give it to someone they know** — mints a `TU-XXXXXX` code that person enters in the app.
3. **Release it to the scholarship pool** — when someone writes to say they cannot pay, the month
   comes from the pool rather than from the founder's pocket.

Option 3 is the interesting one. *"No one is turned away for money"* was previously a cost the
owner absorbed alone; now practice funds practice. No competitor can copy it, because none of them
verify sittings server-side.

**Redemption grants a comped month directly** (`tier=student`, `active_until` +1 month) rather than
going through Stripe — the recipient may have no card at all, which is the point. If they already
have time remaining, the month is added to the end rather than overwriting it.

**Verified end to end**: giver earned 30 days → code `TU-MDP0CI`; redeeming your own gift →
*"a gift is for someone else"*; an invented code → *"that code is not one of ours"*; the recipient
redeemed successfully and became `student` until 6 Sep; the same code twice → *"that month has
already been taken"*; releasing to the pool → `{months_available: 1, given_by: 1}`. Test accounts
removed; the two real members untouched.

`GET /api/practice/pool` is public — how many months are waiting, and how many practitioners put
them there. That is the honest form of social proof, and worth putting on the Dāna screen.

### Deliberately not built: access in exchange for social posts
Raised and declined. Trading meditation for promotion turns generosity into unpaid marketing and
rewards audience size rather than practice — it is the growth pattern this product is positioned
against. The scholarship route already exists and treats the person as a person.
